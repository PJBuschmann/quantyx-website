# Team headshots

Add one image per person, named with their `id` from `PEOPLE` in
`src/pages/about-contact.jsx`, e.g. `rb.jpg` (Roberto Botter), `ml.jpg` (Michel Lempicki).

- Formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp
- Square crop, face centred, at least 400×400 px, ideally under 150 KB
- Shown as a circle on the People cards and in the bio panel
- No photo → the initials are shown instead
